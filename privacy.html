Privacy Policy

Plate · Last updated October 9, 2026

Plate is built to keep your data on your phone. There is no sign-up, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is ever sold.

What stays on your phone

Your food log, meals, water, vitamins, weight, goals, profile and settings are stored only on your device. Plate does not upload them, and we cannot see them.

Deleting the app deletes this data. You can export a copy, or erase everything, at any time from Settings.

The AI Coach (only when you ask)

When you send the Coach a question, Plate sends that question plus a short summary of the relevant numbers (such as your daily targets and what you've logged) to the Plate server, which passes it to our AI provider, Anthropic, to write the answer. You can see exactly what is shared from inside the Coach.

The Plate server does not store your questions or the answers. It keeps only:

  • a random account number made on your phone's first Coach use (no name, email or phone number),
  • your Coach credit balance, and
  • a record of each question's cost (date, AI model and size), so balances add up.

Anthropic processes the request to produce the answer and does not use it to train its models. Our server runs on Cloudflare.

Barcode scanning

When you scan a barcode, Plate looks up that product's barcode number on Open Food Facts, a free public food database. Only the barcode number is sent. The camera image never leaves your phone.

Fonts

Plate loads its headline font from Google Fonts, which means Google can see a request from your device's internet address. No personal data is included.

Children

Plate is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
